# Session 01 – Foundations of Bioinformatics

## Overview

This session introduced the fundamental concepts of bioinformatics, including its history, nucleotide structure, IUPAC nucleotide codes, and the evolution of DNA sequencing technologies.

---

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the concept of bioinformatics.
- Review the history and evolution of bioinformatics.
- Learn nucleotide structure.
- Understand IUPAC nucleotide ambiguity codes.
- Compare DNA sequencing technologies.
- Understand the principles of Sanger and Maxam–Gilbert sequencing.

---

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to Bioinformatics
- History of Bioinformatics
- Bioinformatics Eras
- Nucleotide Structure
- IUPAC Codes
- DNA Sequencing
- Sanger Sequencing
- Maxam–Gilbert Sequencing
- Overview of Next-Generation Sequencing
